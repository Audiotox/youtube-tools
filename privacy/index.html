Privacy policy for youtube-tools

Last updated 13 October 2026.

Who uses it

youtube-tools is single-user: its author is its only user. There is no sign-up, no account, no server and no service for anyone else.

What it does

It runs on the author's own computer. Through the YouTube Data API, signed in as the author, it uploads audio digests the author has made to the author's own YouTube channel, and manages playlists on the author's own channels (adding, removing and ordering videos).

Google data it uses

It asks Google only for the YouTube permissions those two jobs need. It reads and changes nothing outside the author's own YouTube account.

What it stores

No data about anyone. The only thing it keeps is the sign-in token Google issues to the author, stored on the author's computer and used only for these jobs.

Who it talks to

Only Google's YouTube Data API, through Google's sign-in. No third parties, no analytics, no advertising, and no sharing or selling of data.

Its use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Its use of YouTube is under the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

The author can remove youtube-tools at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which ends its access at once.

This website

Static pages on GitHub Pages. They set no cookies and run no scripts.

Contact

The author, through the GitHub repository at github.com/Audiotox/youtube-tools.
